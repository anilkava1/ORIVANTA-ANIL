# LEGACY – Ramayan / Indian mythic-era character bible (use only if a script covers Ramayan/Mahabharat era; same face construction as the main cast)

Style note: all characters are **flat 2D cut-out puppets** (head, torso, upper/lower arms, legs as separate jointed pieces) with thin dark-brown outlines, subtle paper drop-shadow, and rich gold jewellery. Faces: large calm almond eyes, half-lidded, small smile, tiny nose, smooth cheeks. Proportion: tall, slim, long arms, ~7 heads tall. Always use the **same face construction** for every character; differentiate by colour, crown, beard, props.

> Source: Ram, Krishna, Sita, Lakshman, Vishwamitra, Vali/Sugriva, Bhishma, Dasharatha, queens, Vashishtha, Narakasura, Shakuni, Kevat-style villagers were observed in the reference video. Characters marked **(extension)** were NOT in the reference video; they are designed in the same style following standard Indian iconography so the cast is complete.

Reusable formula for every character prompt:
`[Name], flat 2D paper-cutout Indian devotional animation style, [skin], [crown], [clothes], [jewellery], [props], calm half-lidded eyes, thin brown outline, soft drop shadow, front/three-quarter view, full body, plain background`

---
## Shri Ram (core)
- **Skin:** sky/cerulean blue (lighter than Krishna). Slight lighter highlight on cheeks/chest.
- **Crown (mukut):** tall gold conical, tiered, red/green gem at centre, small ornament on top.
- **Face:** serene, eyes lightly lowered, gentle closed smile. Tilak: thin vertical U-shaped Vaishnava mark in white/red on forehead.
- **Upper body:** bare chest; layered gold necklaces with central gem pendant; green + red flower garland (vanamala) hanging to the knees; gold armbands (bajuband) + bracelets; **pink/red sash (angvastra)** hanging off both arms/shoulders.
- **Lower body:** **orange dhoti** with gold woven border and small motif; gold waist-belt (kamarbandh).
- **Props:** tall **dhanush (bow)** gold-brown, with string, held vertical in left hand; quiver of arrows on back (peeking above shoulder).
- **Pose language:** straight spine, right hand in *abhaya* (blessing) or at chest; weight even; never aggressive. In war: bow drawn but face stays calm.
- **Aura:** soft **golden sun-disc/halo** behind head; optional thin gold circle ring around body.
- **Exile (vanvaas) variant:** same face; **no crown → jata-mukut (matted hair bun)**, tree-bark/deerskin kati-vastra, minimal jewellery, bow kept.
- **Ayodhya coronation variant:** full gold jewellery, white-gold dhoti, royal umbrella (chhatra).

## Sita Mata
- **Skin:** warm golden-wheat. Gentle, radiant.
- **Crown:** smaller gold crown with a **single central drop** (maang-tikka), long black braid/hair behind.
- **Clothes:** **red choli/blouse**, **yellow saree with gold floral print and gold border**, green/red sash/dupatta over arm, gold necklaces, earrings, bangles.
- **Face:** soft, eyes lowered, modest; hand raised in blessing.
- **Special variants:** *Golden Sita (Agni/golden form)* = entire figure glowing gold with sparks. *Vanvaas* = simple yellow saree, flowers in hair. *Ashok Vatika* = pale yellow, no heavy jewellery, tearful but serene.

## Lakshman
- **Skin:** warm golden-brown / light tan (warmer than Sita).
- **Crown:** shorter gold crown. 
- **Clothes:** orange-yellow dhoti, green + pink sashes on arms, gold ornaments, quiver on back.
- **Props:** bow, always one step behind Ram, eyes alert, slightly stern but loyal. 
- **Posture:** hands folded/ready to serve.

## Hanuman (extension)
- **Skin/fur:** saffron-orange-golden fur, red face with vanara features (short snout, expressive big eyes), long tail curling upward with tuft.
- **Clothes:** red langot/short dhoti with gold border, gold armlets, necklace, small crown (mukut) with gem, **Ram-naam** written on chest/necklace (Devanagari "राम").
- **Props:** **gada (mace)** gold-silver on shoulder; Dronagiri mountain in hand for Sanjeevani scenes.
- **Pose:** *namaskar/hands folded* is default (bhakti), or flying with fist forward. Chest-open "Ram in heart" variant: chest is open showing Ram–Sita (devotional glow).
- **Size:** scalable (small → giant in Lanka-jump & Virat-roop scenes).

## Ravan (extension)
- **Skin:** deep bluish-grey/dark maroon-brown.
- **Heads:** **10 heads** in two arcs (centre head larger, 5 left/5 right), each with gold crown; stylised, not scary. Only the central face shows expression clearly. 20 arms optional in battle.
- **Clothes:** dark red + black dhoti, heavy gold jewellery, large moustache, gold shoulder pieces. 
- **Mood:** proud/regal; menace by colour (dark red, black, ember glow), not by gore.
- **Note:** show his scholar side too (Shiva devotion, veena) when script says so.

## Dasharath
- Elderly king; **white beard**, fair-tan skin, gold crown with red gem, white-gold dhoti, rich necklaces; tired/soft eyes. Seen in palace arches. Sits on throne.

## Kaushalya / Sumitra / Kaikeyi
- Queens in **saree + choli** (Kaushalya: pink/red + gold; Sumitra: green/orange; Kaikeyi: deep red/maroon with darker, sharper-yet-still-flat styling). Small gold crowns, tikka, bangles. Three queens stand in a row in palace scenes. Kaikeyi's sadness/anger shown through colours & posture, not ugliness.

## Bharat & Shatrughna (extension)
- Same family look as Lakshman, skin tones tan/golden. **Bharat in nandigram:** jata, simple robes, holds **Ram's paduka (wooden sandals)** on head/throne.

## Sugriv & Vali (observed)
- Vanara kings: brown face with **lion-like mane and beard**, bright eyes, gold crown, maroon/red dhoti (Vali), blue-grey dhoti (Sugriv), gold ornaments. Vali bigger and darker; Sugriv slightly lighter.

## Jambavan, Angad, Jatayu, Sampati (extension)
- **Jambavan:** old bear-king, dark brown fur, white beard patch, gold necklace, staff.
- **Angad:** young vanara, light brown, gold armlets (angad), firm stance.
- **Jatayu:** large golden-brown vulture/eagle, white head ruff, gold collar, fallen bravely; soft light on death scene.

## Rishis (observed)
- **Vishwamitra:** tall matted hair bun on top, white beard, brown skin, orange robes, rudraksha, raised-hand blessing. 
- **Vashishtha:** seated white-robed sage, white beard, in lotus pose, light rays from above.
- Others: Agastya, Valmiki (ashram, with pen/palm-leaf), Bharadwaj, Atri/Anasuya. Keep **rudraksha + orange/white + beard + jata**.

## Shabari, Kevat (Nishadraj), Ahilya, Vibhishan, Kumbhkaran, Meghnad, Surpanakha, Maricha (extension)
- **Shabari:** old tribal woman, white hair, simple orange saree, holding bowl of berries; eyes wet with devotion.
- **Kevat:** boatman, dark-brown skin, simple dhoti, oar, folded hands (washes Ram's feet – reverence scene).
- **Vibhishan:** same face as Ravan family but **one head**, calm, blue-gold dress, folded hands.
- **Kumbhkaran:** giant, grey-brown skin, huge body, sleepy heavy eyes, small crown.
- **Meghnad (Indrajit):** one head, dark skin, armour, bow, blue-black clothes.
- **Surpanakha / Maricha (golden deer):** Maricha-deer = flat golden deer with white spots and glowing gold aura, slightly *too* shiny.

## Crowd / villagers (observed)
- Rows of identical cut-out people in **bright coloured sarees/dhotis (magenta, orange, green, yellow)**, hands folded, bobbing slightly. Use for praja of Ayodhya, welcome of Ram, vanar-sena.

## Vanar Sena
- Many small vanaras, brown/orange fur, holding rocks/trees; used for Ram Setu (rocks with "राम" written float).

## Props library (same design always)
Ram's dhanush · arrows with feathered tails · quiver · Hanuman's gada · lotus · diya (clay lamp with gold flame) · paduka · Sanjeevani mountain · Ram Setu stones with "राम" · Pushpak Vimana (golden floral chariot) · Ayodhya palace (cream/gold domes with orange flags) · Panchavati hut (leaf-thatched) · Lanka (dark-red/black fortress with gold) · Sarayu river (blue wavy lines).
