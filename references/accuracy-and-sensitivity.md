# Accuracy & Sensitivity Rules (mandatory)

## Accuracy
1. **Dates & numbers:** use the script's figures; if unsure, search reputable sources (museums, universities, encyclopaedias) or mark `[verify]`. Say "≈" for estimates.
2. **No impossible meetings:** non-avian dinosaurs and humans never overlap. Cross-era shots only as labelled *scale comparison* or *imagination*.
3. **Pterosaurs, mosasaurs, ichthyosaurs are not dinosaurs** – label correctly. Birds *are* living dinosaurs.
4. **Anachronism check:** clothes, tools, buildings, animals, crops must exist in that time/place (e.g., no potatoes in medieval Europe, no horses in pre-Columbian Americas, no stirrups in early Rome).
5. **Disputed topics** (origin of Indo-Aryans, Indus script, Aryan migration, exact fire dates, etc.): present as "historians debate", show both options, never as settled unless the script/sources say so.
6. **Myth vs history:** label myths/legends clearly ("कथा के अनुसार / According to tradition"). Treat epics (Ramayan, Mahabharat, Iliad…) as cultural heritage; depict with the same respect as history, labelled as tradition.
7. **Maps & borders:** use era-appropriate names/regions; avoid modern borders for ancient scenes unless labelled.

## Respect & sensitivity
1. **Every culture/religion with dignity.** No caricature (skin tone exaggeration, accents-as-jokes, costume stereotypes). Show diversity inside each society (ages, genders, classes).
2. **Sacred figures & symbols:** depict only with care; avoid disrespectful context; avoid swastika (as hate symbol) misuse – if showing the ancient/Indic auspicious sign, label context; no Nazi imagery beyond what's essential and always restrained.
3. **Violence & tragedy** (wars, Partition, slavery, genocide, colonial atrocities): symbolic, restrained visuals (silhouettes, empty places, objects, weather); **no gore, no graphic harm to children**. Honour victims; avoid "scoreboard" tone.
4. **Colonialism, caste, gender, religion:** be factual; present perspectives fairly; do not editorialise unless the script does.
5. **Real people:** stylised cut-outs from historical records; avoid making real people look foolish; use name ribbons; avoid private individuals.
6. **No celebrity/actor likenesses** and no copied artwork; reference *style* only.
7. **Children's safety:** friendly creatures, no scary jump-cuts.
8. **Copyright:** do not copy other creators' characters/layouts; maintain original designs.
9. **Disclaimers (optional line):** "इतिहास अलग-अलग स्रोतों पर आधारित है; तारीखें लगभग हैं।"
