# Art Style Guide (the fixed "look")

Derived from the reference video style; applies to every era.

## Core look
- **Flat 2D vector / paper-cutout puppet animation.** Layered pieces with a faint soft drop-shadow (papercraft depth). Thin dark-brown outlines on characters/creatures.
- **Gold ornamental accents** everywhere: frames, ribbons, chapter cards, text outlines. Era-appropriate pattern borders can replace folk motifs (see palette table).
- **3–4 depth layers** per scene: sky/light → far shapes → mid architecture/nature → ground (+ foreground frame pieces such as leaves, arches, ropes).
- **Light & glow:** sun/moon disc, light rays, vignette, sparkles, drifting particles (ash, pollen, sand, snow, embers, petals, paper, pixels depending on era).
- Characters stand in front of simple, richly coloured backgrounds. No photo-realism, no 3D, no gradients-heavy glossy look.

## Signature graphic elements (always available, used when the script needs them)
- **Chapter card:** dark navy/purple panel, ornate gold border + corner ornaments, small chapter label, huge Devanagari word, tiny English translation, motif icons left/right (**change motif per video & per era**: fossil + comet, flint + fire, wheat + sickle, scroll + quill, wheel + gear, rocket + satellite…).
- **Name ribbon:** small ribbon banner above a character/creature at first appearance (colour by culture/era).
- **Date/era stamp:** ribbon or sign with year ("≈ 66 मिलियन साल पहले", "3300 BCE", "1857").
- **VS split-screen:** vertical gold divider with VS medallion; two palettes, two sets of icons, tick/cross comparisons.
- **Counters & dials:** big number + dotted ring/segment fill, tally marks, bar growth.
- **Subtitles:** bottom centre, white bold with black outline; **spoken word highlighted yellow**.
- **Tick / cross marks, question mark pop, stamp, speech bubble** for myth-vs-fact style beats.

## Typography
- Devanagari display: bold calligraphic, gold-yellow, dark outline + shadow.
- Small caps sans (spaced) for English sub-labels. Keep ≤ 7 words on screen at once for headlines.
- Safe margins: 5% edges; 15% bottom for subtitles (9:16: keep subtitles in lower-middle, away from UI).

## Motion language (cut-out animation)
- Characters move by **joint rotation** (arm raise, head tilt, breathing bob, blink, walk-cycle by leg swing). Creatures: jaw open/close, tail sway, wing flap, leg pistons. No realistic morphing.
- Entrances: slide + ease-out, pop-scale, drop-in with bounce. Exits: fade/slide.
- Camera: push-in on emotion, pull-back for scale reveals, pan for travel, parallax between layers, vertical tilt for "going down in time".
- Effects loops: rotating rays, flickering fire, drifting particles, water lines, twinkling stars, smoke columns.
- Beat length 3–8 s. Vary shot size every 2–3 beats.

## Colour rules
- 3–4 dominant colours per scene + one accent (gold by default).
- Never put a character on a background of the same hue without rim light/outline/contrast shape.
- Palette shifts must follow **meaning shifts** in the script (e.g. green → ember at extinction; fire-orange → daylight gold at agriculture).
- Across a video, keep a **recurring accent colour or motif** so it feels like one series.

## Variation rule
Every new script gets: a new *key-colour story*, new set of locations, new chapter-card motifs, new camera/pacing. Only the cut-out look + construction rules persist.

## Aspect ratios
16:9 (1920×1080) default; 9:16 (1080×1920) for Shorts/Reels: stack elements vertically, bigger characters, headline near top third.
