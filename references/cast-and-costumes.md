# Cast & Costume System (build ANY person/creature in the same style)

## Human construction (locked for all eras)
- Flat 2D **cut-out puppet**: head, torso, upper/lower arms, hands, legs as separate jointed pieces; thin dark-brown outline; soft paper drop-shadow.
- ≈ 7 heads tall for adults, 5 for children, slim, long arms. Large calm almond eyes (half-lidded), small nose, small smile, smooth cheeks. **Same face construction for everyone**; identity comes from skin tone, hair, headwear, clothes, props.
- Expression via brows + mouth + eyelids only. Keep dignified; humour via posture/prop, never caricature of a race/culture.
- Skin palette (use all, according to place/time): `#5b3a29 · #8d5a3b · #b97a4b · #d9a066 · #e8b57a · #f1c9a0 · #f6dcc0`. Ancient populations were diverse – do not default to one tone.
- Hair kit: tight curls · long straight · braids · topknot · bald · bun · veil/headcloth · wigs (Egypt, 18th-c Europe) · hair-band.
- Formula for any character prompt:
  `[Name/role], flat 2D paper-cutout animation style, [skin tone], [hair/headwear], [clothing by era], [jewellery/tools], calm half-lidded eyes, thin brown outline, soft drop shadow, full body, plain background`

## Crowds
Rows of identical cut-outs with **varied colours** of clothing, slight bobbing; use for markets, armies, protests, audiences, workers, pilgrims. Add 2–3 hero figures among them with unique props.

## Creature kit (dinosaurs & ancient animals) – same cut-out look
- Build from big simple shapes with outline: body ellipse, neck/tail tapered paths, legs as jointed pieces, head separate (jaw hinge).
- Colour with 2–3 flat tones + pattern (stripes, spots, crest), subtle belly lighter tone. Friendly-but-accurate proportions; **no gore, no blood, no scary close-ups**.
- Show feathers where accurate (many theropods e.g. Velociraptor were feathered; T. rex likely had scaly skin with possible limited feathering – hedge in notes).
- Scale cues: put a tiny human-sized silhouette **only in clearly labelled "scale comparison" beats**, never "living together".
- Core cast: Triceratops, T. rex, Velociraptor, Brachiosaurus/Diplodocus, Stegosaurus, Ankylosaurus, Pterosaurs (Quetzalcoatlus – *not* dinosaurs), marine reptiles (Mosasaurus, Ichthyosaur – *not* dinosaurs), early mammals (shrew-like), dragonflies, ferns, cycads, conifers.
- Later animals: sabre-tooth cat, woolly mammoth, giant sloth, aurochs, wolves → dogs, horses, cattle, camels, elephants, ships' rats, silk moths.
- Extinction beats: asteroid streak, shock-ring, ash sky, silhouettes lying down gently – stylised, no gore.

## Name ribbons
Red/gold = rulers & leaders · teal = scientists & thinkers · green = farmers/nature · indigo = explorers · saffron = spiritual teachers · grey-brown = workers/soldiers · creature ribbons = rust/orange.

## Recurring-guide option (optional)
A small neutral "time-traveller" guide character (same construction, simple coat/scarf, backpack with a clock) can appear across eras if the script benefits from a host; skip if the script is pure narration.
