# Eras & Settings Reference (dinosaur → today)

Dates are approximate and sometimes disputed – verify with sources when the script gives specific claims; mark `[verify]` if not verified.
Use as inspiration for *what could appear*, not a mandatory order. Scripts may cover one era, jump, loop, or compare.

## Deep time
- **Triassic (≈252–201 mya):** early dinosaurs, dry continent Pangaea, red rocks, ferns/cycads.
- **Jurassic (≈201–145 mya):** giants (Brachiosaurus, Diplodocus), Stegosaurus, early birds (Archaeopteryx); lush forests, warm shallow seas.
- **Cretaceous (≈145–66 mya):** T. rex, Triceratops, flowering plants, bees; marine reptiles & pterosaurs share the world (not dinosaurs).
- **Extinction (≈66 mya):** asteroid (Chicxulub) + volcanism; ash sky, shock wave, cooling; survivors: small mammals, birds (living dinosaurs), crocodilians.
- **Mammal age:** forests → grasslands; primates appear; whales evolve; Ice Ages later.
- ⚠ Non-avian dinosaurs and humans never coexisted (~66 million years apart).

## Human story
- **Hominins (≈4–2 mya):** Australopithecus (Lucy), upright walking; savanna.
- **Homo habilis / erectus (≈2.4–1 mya):** stone tools; control of fire (≥ ~1 mya, evidence debated); migration out of Africa.
- **Homo sapiens (≈300,000 yrs ago):** Africa; later spread; Neanderthals/Denisovans cousins; interbreeding.
- **Cognitive/Art (≈50–40 kya onward):** cave paintings (Sulawesi, Chauvet, Lascaux), hand stencils, beads, flutes.
- **Ice Age (ends ≈11,700 yrs ago):** mammoths, sabre-tooths, mammoth-bone huts, land bridges.
- **Agricultural Revolution (≈10,000 BCE):** wheat/barley (Fertile Crescent), rice (Yangtze, India), maize (Mesoamerica), domestic animals; first villages (Çatalhöyük, Mehrgarh).

## Early civilisations
- **Mesopotamia (≈3500–500 BCE):** Sumer, writing (≈3300 BCE), wheel, ziggurats, Hammurabi's laws.
- **Egypt (≈3100 BCE→):** Nile, pyramids (Giza ≈2600 BCE), hieroglyphs, mummification, daily-life of builders.
- **Indus Valley (≈3300–1300 BCE):** Harappa/Mohenjo-daro, planned cities, drainage, seals, Lothal dockyard.
- **China:** Yellow River, Shang oracle bones, Zhou, Qin unification (221 BCE), Han, Silk Road, paper, compass, printing.
- **Bronze Age exchange / Late Bronze collapse (≈1200 BCE).**

## Classical & imperial
- **Persia (Achaemenid), Greece (Athens, Sparta, Alexander), Rome (Republic → Empire).**
- **India:** Vedic age, Mahajanapadas, Buddha/Mahavira, Maurya (Chandragupta, Ashoka), Gupta golden age (zero, astronomy, Ajanta).
- **Americas:** Olmec, Maya (calendar, cities), Teotihuacan, Inca roads/quipu, Aztec Tenochtitlan.
- **Africa:** Kush, Aksum, Mali (Mansa Musa), Great Zimbabwe, Swahili coast.
- **Silk Road & Indian Ocean trade:** goods, religions, ideas, diseases travelling.

## Medieval → early modern
- **Islamic golden age:** House of Wisdom, algebra, optics, medicine, astronomy.
- **Medieval Europe, Vikings, Crusades, Black Death.**
- **Mongol Empire:** Genghis Khan, Pax Mongolica, postal relays.
- **South Asia:** Delhi Sultanate, Vijayanagara, Chola navy, Mughals (Akbar–Shah Jahan), Marathas, Sikh Gurus.
- **East Asia:** Tang/Song/Ming, Heian Japan, Samurai, Joseon Korea.
- **Renaissance, Printing (≈1450), Age of Exploration, Columbian Exchange, Scientific Revolution.**

## Modern
- **Industrial Revolution (≈1760→)**, railways, telegraph, urbanisation, labour movements.
- **Colonialism & resistance, 1857, independence movements, Abolition.**
- **World War I & II (restrained, no gore), Cold War, decolonisation, Partition (sensitive).**
- **Space age (Sputnik 1957, Gagarin 1961, Moon 1969, Chandrayaan, Mars missions).**
- **Digital age:** computers, internet (1990s), mobile, social media, AI; green energy & climate; today's culture (festivals, food, music, cinema, sports).
